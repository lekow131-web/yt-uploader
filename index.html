yt-uploader

yt-uploader is a small private command-line tool. Its owner uses it to publish videos to their own YouTube channels through the official YouTube Data API v3.

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's own video files to the owner's own channels.
  • Sets the title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions, playlists, scheduled publish time and the altered/synthetic content disclosure of those videos.
  • Reads basic information about the owner's own channels (channel name and the list of the owner's uploads) to make sure a video goes to the right channel.

Who can use it

Only the owner of the channels. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and does not access any other person's account or data.
